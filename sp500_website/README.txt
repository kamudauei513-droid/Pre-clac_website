SP500 exponential growth website

Files:
- index.html: page content and section structure
- style.css: styling, glass buttons, animated background, responsive layout
- script.js: calculator, graph, scroll reveals, mobile menu
- assets/background.jpg: extracted from your PowerPoint slide background

How to preview:
1. Extract the ZIP folder.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser. The chart uses Chart.js from a CDN, so an internet connection is needed for the graph.
3. Edit the text in index.html, colors and animation in style.css, and calculations/interactions in script.js.

Note: The historical average is presented as the arithmetic average shown in your slides. The model is illustrative and is not a market forecast.
